## Archive status

Year: `1965`

This index is the navigation record for the Korea annual statistical archive. Each domain document is maintained separately so that units, definitions, source years, and historical breaks can be documented without silently combining unlike series.

`N/A` means that a comparable observation has not been verified for the year; it does not mean zero.

## Methods — WHY

The index provides a stable entry point for academic, professional, institutional, historical, and analytical use. Domain-level separation preserves provenance and makes later source transcription auditable.

## Sources

Primary source families include KOSIS national statistics and MAFRA/KASS agricultural statistics. International series may be used as secondary cross-checks where definitions are comparable. KOSIS provides Agriculture and Forestry and Fishery as dedicated statistical subjects and exposes statistical tables and metadata through its statistical database.
